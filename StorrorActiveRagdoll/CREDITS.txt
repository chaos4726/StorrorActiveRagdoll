  reduced to about 39,000 triangles, their faces turned outward, and each bone fitted onto STORROR's character
  skeleton (scaled, turned and moved to its joints) to be drawn as an in-game x-ray. The original OBJ files
  carry an older license note (CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan); the database's current license, last
  updated 2025-02-25, is CC Attribution 4.0 International, which is the one followed here.
